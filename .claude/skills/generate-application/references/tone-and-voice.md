# Tone and Voice Reference

## Cover Letter Voice

- **Direct and evidence-first.** Open with the strongest proof point, not flattery. No "I am excited to apply for…" openers.
- **Warm, helpful, and audience-first.** Show genuine interest in the company's mission through a specific observation, and frame your value as how you will help the team solve real problems.
- **Always include a greeting.** Start with a clear greeting line such as "Dear Hiring Manager," or "Dear [Team Name] Team," before paragraph one.
- **First person, active voice.** "I built", "I led", "I shipped" - not "was responsible for" or "helped with".
- **Two paragraphs, hard limit.** Paragraph 1: who you are + the one thing that makes you the right fit. Paragraph 2: why this company/role specifically, and how your experience helps the team now.
- **Avoid AI-slop punctuation.** Do not use em dashes in cover letters. Prefer plain punctuation like commas and periods.
- **Use a concise close.** End with a brief, professional closing line such as "Sincerely," without filler or long pleasantries.
- **No buzzword padding.** Cut "passionate", "dynamic", "synergy", "leverage", "thought leader". Every adjective needs a fact behind it.
- **Never volunteer a gap unprompted.** Gap honesty belongs in `analysis.md` (internal) and in factual screener answers when the form directly asks. The cover letter itself sells fit — do not write sentences like "my experience has been X rather than Y, so I'd be building on that muscle." ML6 rejected a 2026-07-17 application citing the exact gap ("external stakeholder management/client communication") the cover letter volunteered unprompted in paragraph 2. If a real gap exists, either omit it from the letter or reframe it as adjacent proof (nearest evidence you do have), never as a disclaimer.

## CV Profile Statement Tone

- **First person, metric-led.** Start with years of experience and a concrete system shipped, not a job title.
- **Domain specificity over generality.** Name the stack, the scale, the outcome. "Architected Python/FastAPI RAG microservices deployed on Kubernetes" beats "experienced in backend development".
- **Max 4 sentences.** Opening scope → key technical achievement → key leadership/research achievement → differentiator or domain angle.
- **No self-assessments.** Never "strong communicator" or "quick learner". Let the proof points speak.

## Employment Bullet Tone

- **Formula:** Action verb → system/scope → measurable outcome (where available).
  - Good: "Architected a Rust-based embeddings API clustering research-document vectors with parallel K-means — deployed as a Kubernetes microservice serving live traffic."
  - Bad: "Worked on improving the embeddings pipeline to be faster."
- **Present tense for current role, past tense for previous roles.**
- **Name the tech.** Mention languages, frameworks, and infrastructure used — it helps automated screening and shows depth.
- **One idea per bullet.** Do not combine two achievements in one sentence with "and also".
- **Metrics where real.** Use actual numbers from the profile. Do not invent or round up.
- **No passive voice.** "Delivered", "Built", "Led", "Shipped", "Designed" — not "was involved in" or "contributed to".
