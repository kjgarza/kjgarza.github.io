## Search Strategy by Tier

### Tier 1 — Apply proactively even without perfect role match
- Monitor career pages weekly
- Tailor positioning to "AI + research infrastructure" intersection
- Lead with production LLM systems + publications record

### Tier 2 — Apply to open roles, reach out to hiring managers
- Lead with DataCite/PID/FAIR domain expertise
- Differentiate with AI/LLM engineering skills (inverted positioning)
- Leverage existing community connections

### Tier 3 — Apply selectively to strong matches
- Focus on roles where the intersection creates unusual fit
- Lead with the most relevant proof point per company
