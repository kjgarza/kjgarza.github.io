# Tone and Voice Reference — Customer Success / Account Management

Profile: `claudia`. Same discipline as the engineering profile, different evidence: the
proof is relationships, retention, and revenue rather than systems shipped.

## Cover Letter Voice

- **Direct and evidence-first.** Open with the strongest proof point, not flattery. No "I am excited to apply for…" openers.
- **Warm, helpful, and audience-first.** Show genuine interest in the company through a specific observation about their customers or product, and frame value as how you will help the team.
- **Always include a greeting.** Start with a clear greeting line such as "Dear Hiring Manager," or "Dear [Team] Team," before paragraph one.
- **First person, active voice.** "I owned", "I led", "I grew" — not "was responsible for" or "helped with".
- **Two paragraphs, hard limit.** Paragraph 1: who you are plus the one thing that makes you the right fit. Paragraph 2: why this company and role specifically, and how your experience helps the team now.
- **Avoid AI-slop punctuation.** No em dashes. Plain commas and periods.
- **Use a concise close.** "Sincerely," and the name. No long pleasantries.
- **No buzzword padding.** Cut "passionate", "dynamic", "synergy", "leverage", "customer-obsessed". Every adjective needs a fact behind it.
- **Never volunteer a gap unprompted.** Gap honesty belongs in `analysis.md` (internal) and in factual screener answers when the form directly asks. The letter sells fit. Do not write "my background is X rather than Y". If a real gap exists, either omit it or reframe as the nearest adjacent evidence you actually have.
- **Language is a differentiator, not a footnote.** For DACH-facing roles, state German, English, and Spanish plainly in paragraph two.

## CV Profile Statement Tone

- **First person, metric-led.** Start with years of experience and the size or shape of the book of business, not a job title.
- **Scope specificity over generality.** Name the segment, the ARR, the outcome. "Primary strategic advisor to a €6.5M ARR enterprise portfolio" beats "experienced account manager".
- **Max 4 sentences.** Scope → commercial achievement → relationship or cross-functional achievement → differentiator (languages, domain).
- **No self-assessments.** Never "strong communicator", "relationship builder", or "team player". Let the proof points speak.

## Employment Bullet Tone

- **Formula:** Action verb → scope or portfolio → measurable outcome (where available).
  - Good: "Led Executive Business Reviews for a €6.5M ARR enterprise portfolio, aligning customer objectives with platform capabilities and surfacing expansion opportunities."
  - Bad: "Was responsible for building strong relationships with customers and helping them succeed."
- **Present tense for the current role, past tense for previous roles.**
- **Name the platform and the segment.** Braze, Navan, Enterprise, Mid-Market, DACH. It helps automated screening and shows depth.
- **One idea per bullet.** Do not join two achievements with "and also".
- **Metrics where real.** €6.5M ARR, 80%+ YoY, 50% opt-in lift. Do not invent or round up.
- **No passive voice.** "Owned", "Led", "Grew", "Built", "Delivered".
