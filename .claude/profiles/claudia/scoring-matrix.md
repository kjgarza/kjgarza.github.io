# Scoring Matrix — Customer Success / Account Management

Profile: `claudia`. Dimensions and weights differ from an engineering profile: the shape of
the book of business and the language requirement matter more than a tech stack.

**Every dimension scores the fit between Claudia's evidenced experience and what the posting
asks for.** None of them scores how attractive the role is on its own. A role can be a
perfect Senior CSM posting in a great company and still score badly here because she has
never done that specific motion or domain. Scoring role attributes instead of fit is the
failure mode this matrix is built to avoid: it produces 100% for every well-titled job.

## Multi-Dimensional Score (100%)

| Dimension | Weight | Scoring Guide |
|-----------|--------|---------------|
| **Core function** | 25% | Score the motion she has actually performed, not the job title. Post-sales book of business she has run before (SaaS CSM/AM renewals, adoption, expansion) = 100, same motion in a different commercial model (media/ads book, marketplace supply, agency retainer) = 70, mixed CS plus onboarding or support = 65, pure onboarding or implementation = 50, support or service desk = 20, pure new-business hunting AE = 35 |
| **Requirement overlap** | 20% | (requirements she can evidence / total listed requirements) × 100. Count both required and preferred bullets. A requirement she has never done scores 0 for that bullet even when adjacent experience exists; adjacency is argued in the analysis, not scored here. This is the dimension that catches domain and tooling gaps the other rows would otherwise hide |
| **Domain alignment** | 15% | Score her evidenced domain experience against the posting's domain. B2B SaaS, martech, CRM = 100, e-commerce and D2C = 85, mobile gaming = 80, travel and expense = 80, fintech = 55, adtech and paid media = 30 (no evidenced experience), regulated or deep-tech verticals = 25 |
| **Seniority fit** | 15% | Senior / Lead IC = 100, Team Lead or Manager = 85, Director = 60, Mid-level = 40, Junior = 0 |
| **Commercial scope** | 10% | Compare the posting's book to the one she has run. Enterprise or Mid-Market named accounts with renewal and expansion ownership = 100, named accounts without commercial ownership = 70, hard-quota carrying with a personal revenue number = 65 (she has influenced revenue, not carried a quota), SMB or pooled book = 45 |
| **Language and location** | 10% | German + English required, Berlin or hybrid Berlin = 100, English-only Berlin = 85, remote EU = 85, hybrid Hamburg/Munich = 70, relocation required = 30 |
| **Growth signal** | 5% | Clear expansion or strategy remit, exec exposure, or new-market build = 100, steady-state account maintenance = 40 |

### Worked example (regression anchor)

Sr. Client Account Manager, DACH Retail at Pinterest, scored 2026-08-13:

| Dimension | Weight | Score | Why |
|-----------|--------|-------|-----|
| Core function | 25% | 70 | Owns a book, but the motion is a media/ads book, not SaaS post-sales |
| Requirement overlap | 20% | 70 | 7 of 10 listed bullets evidenced; digital ad sales, a Search/Shopping/Display/Social foundation, and demonstrated AI-tool use are not |
| Domain alignment | 15% | 30 | Adtech, no evidenced experience; retail customers are the adjacent part |
| Seniority fit | 15% | 100 | Senior IC |
| Commercial scope | 10% | 65 | Ads team implies a carried number; she has influenced revenue |
| Language and location | 10% | 100 | German plus English, Berlin hybrid |
| Growth signal | 5% | 100 | Named partner book with cross-sell and upsell remit |
| **Total** | | **73%** | STRETCH |

An earlier hand-scored pass on the same posting, before this matrix existed, produced 68% /
STRETCH. Agreement within a few points is the point of the anchor. If a rescoring lands far
from 73%, the matrix has drifted — check whether a dimension has started scoring the role
instead of the fit.

## Recommendation Thresholds

| Score | Recommendation |
|-------|----------------|
| 80%+  | GO — strong match, apply confidently |
| 60-79%| STRETCH — good match with gaps, apply with honest framing |
| <60%  | PASS — significant misalignment, not worth applying |

## Knockout Checks

Score is capped at 55% (PASS) if any of these hold:

- Requires a language other than German, English, or Spanish at working proficiency.
- Requires relocation outside Berlin/Hamburg/Munich with no remote option.
- Requires visa sponsorship the employer does not offer.
- Is an individual-contributor role below Senior level.
